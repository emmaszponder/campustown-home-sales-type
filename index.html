Dream Campus Communities

Exploring which college towns offer the best combination of affordability, home size, community resources, and access to major universities.

Research Question

Which college towns provide the strongest balance of affordability, housing space, and proximity to educational opportunity?


Universities are often economic and cultural centers for their communities. This project explores housing markets near major universities and asks whether some college towns offer greater value than others.

About the Dataset

The College-Town Home Sales Dataset contains residential real estate transactions from communities located near major university campuses across the United States.

Variables Used

This project focuses on factors that influence housing value near major universities.

  • Sale Price
  • Bedrooms
  • Bathrooms
  • Living Area (Square Feet)
  • Distance to Campus
  • University
  • City and State
  • Year Built

Sales under $10,000 are excluded from analysis to remove unusual transactions and outliers.

Why This Matters

Housing affordability affects students, university employees, families, and long-term residents. Some college towns offer larger homes and lower costs, while others require significantly higher housing investments.


By comparing communities surrounding major universities, this project seeks to identify locations that provide the best overall housing value.

Planned Analyses

  • Compare affordability across university communities.
  • Examine average and median home sale prices.
  • Measure housing value using square footage and price.
  • Explore the relationship between sale price and distance from campus.
  • Identify communities that offer the most space for the money.
  • Develop rankings of Dream Campus Communities.

Future Development

Future versions of this project will include charts, rankings, interactive visualizations, and comparisons between university communities to help identify the best places to live near higher-education institutions.
