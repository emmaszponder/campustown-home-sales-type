College Town Home Sales Dashboard

Exploring 35,442 home sales near 11 university campuses across 11 states.

About the Dataset

This project uses the College Town Home Sales dataset containing residential property sales from communities surrounding major universities. The dataset includes location information, housing characteristics, sale prices, and property attributes.

Variables include city, state, university, sale price, home size, bedrooms, bathrooms, year built, and sale date.

Analysis Rule: Sales below $10,000 are excluded from analysis.
